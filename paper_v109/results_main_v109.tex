\section{Error-controlled spatial explanations in traffic networks}\label{sec:numerics}
Sioux Falls absolute accounts use benchmark flows in veh/h and time units of $0.01$ h; multiply an account by $0.01$ for the hourly vehicle-hour total. Anaheim uses vehicle-minutes per hour. All experiments use the stated fixed-path logit-SUE assignment models and prespecified demand scenarios; shares reported over boundary families are enumeration shares over those families. The adaptive reporting rule of Section~\ref{sec:stability} organizes the experiments: every query either receives one error-controlled common value or an explicit spatial profile. Table~\ref{tab:experimentmap} maps the four studies; they are presented in delivery order --- the compact explanation first, its generating mechanisms second, and the spatial resolution it requires last.
