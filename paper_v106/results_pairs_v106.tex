On the 96 test demand paths, response supervision lowers the median
attribution-column error from 21.18\% to 14.19\% (a relative reduction
of one third) and the geometric-boundary error from 8.74\% to 5.75\%,
while both models retain flow-prediction errors near $0.03\%$ (medians
$0.0322\%$ and $0.0292\%$). The improvement holds in every
active-query group; between group medians, the six-query group has the
largest absolute drop ($8.24$ percentage points) and the single-query
group the largest relative reduction ($39.5\%$). Path-level paired
differences quantify
the uncertainty: the median paired column-error difference is $-5.03$
percentage points with a path-level bootstrap $95\%$ interval of
$[-6.01,-4.48]$ (10,000 resamples), improving 92 of 96 paths; the
paired geometric difference has median $-2.06$ with interval
$[-2.76,-1.65]$, improving 90 of 96. Every group's bootstrap interval
excludes zero, from $[-4.90,-2.95]$ for single-query paths to
$[-8.59,-5.22]$ for six-query paths. Leave-one-path-out medians vary
only within $[-5.11,-4.94]$ (column) and $[-2.11,-2.00]$
(geometric), so no single path carries the result. The
system-contribution difference is secondary and opposite in sign:
median $+0.45$ with interval $[0.01,0.74]$, improving 39 of 96 paths.
Response supervision improves the spatial metrics while both methods
retain system-contribution errors below $3.5\%$ (medians $2.52\%$ and
$3.13\%$); this separation confirms that scalar completeness and
spatial explanation accuracy measure different properties.

On the original 24-path subset, the same-path decomposition in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:coldecomp} identifies demand-response error, rather than
marginal-cost weight error, as the larger component of the attribution
discrepancy: for both models the response component has the larger
norm on all 24 paths after averaging active queries and seeds, with
path-median response and weight-component norms 256.1 and 4.1 under
flow supervision and 158.1 and 3.8 under response supervision.
Component norms need not add because signed components may cancel.
Among the evaluated supervision objectives, response supervision
produced the most consistent gains across active-query groups; the
endpoint-edge objectives give no overall improvement and select the
common initial model for three of five seeds (Appendix~\ref{app:pairs-v102}).

Sign reliability is boundary specific. On the separate frozen six-query
baseline segment of Appendix~\ref{supp:screenlinelearned}, all five models
retain negative accounts at the reference minimizing screenlines for $(1,19)$
and $(7,12)$. Their learned accounts lie in $[-3886.6,-3365.1]$ and
$[-2810.4,-1916.6]$, respectively, against reference values $-2766.6$ and
$-2967.3$. The largest errors over the prescribed screenline families,
1430.8 and 2499.2, are below the corresponding reference sign margins.
These account-specific bounds establish the reported signs while retaining
quantitative magnitude errors. This frozen-segment evidence is separate from
the 96-path comparison above.
